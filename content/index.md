Hi, this is my [Digital Garden](https://jzhao.xyz/posts/networked-thought), partially a collection for hosting various links and information on my professional [research activities](Research/Summary.md) in one place, and partially a walkway along the working of my mind. For the latter, I am collecting lessons I learn while working or otherwise living, and accumulating words to describe my process of growth. 

## What I do for living
I currently work as a Research Engineer at the [Fire Safety Research Institute (FSRI)](https://fsri.org/), a part of [UL Research Institutes](https://ul.org/).

I completed my Ph.D in Mechanical engineering at University of Maryland (UMD), College Park where I researched on the problem of material flammability, pyrolysis, flame spread via multi-scale experiments and modeling. My research journey truly began here.

Prior to that, I completed my Masters in Chemical Engineering at Texas A&M University, College Station. I am from India and completed by bachelors in Chemical Engineering at Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT, Nagpur).

## What I do for life

Backpacking is my way to embrace life. When I cannot backpack, I enjoy macro and landscape photography, music, reading, and [writing about life and living](Journey/Journey.md).
