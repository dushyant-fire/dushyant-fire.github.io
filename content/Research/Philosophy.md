## Research Philosophy

Research often leads to small discoveries, or one could say epiphanies, that brings about big changes in thinking. The pathway to research was a slow discovery for me and I am looking forward to continuing the research process and share it, teach it to any willing student.

I strongly believe that research should combine curiosity to discover something new and the societal needs and individual values. Personally, I am determined to work towards a sustainable future that considers the impact of natural resource utilization and climate change. 

With my experience and background, this determination intersects with a problems related to wild-land fires and threats posed by emerging technologies. 

## Research Interest

I am particularly interested in applying my skills for research in the following three research areas:
- model development for predicting fire spread in wildland-urban interface (WUI) region, 
- modeling of fundamental heat transfer and thermodynamic processes, and
- waste up-cycling via pyrolysis.
